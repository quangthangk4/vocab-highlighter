Backup & Restore

Your words are stored in chrome.storage.sync and automatically sync across devices — they never get deleted when you update the extension.
Use the tools below for manual backup or to migrate between browsers.

Export Words

Download your vocabulary as a plain-text file (one word per line).

Import Words

Import a .txt file (one word per line) or a .json array. Duplicate words are skipped automatically.

⚠️ Danger Zone

Permanently delete all saved words. This cannot be undone.
